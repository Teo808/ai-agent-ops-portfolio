A final answer is only one part of the test. A reliable workflow also shows what it read, what it could do, what changed, and how a person can recover when it stops.

How I use it

I run these cases against a generalized support workflow before calling it ready. I record observable evidence, not just whether the final response sounds good. Each failure should lead to a repair, a clearer handoff, or a deliberate stop.

This is the practical layer between AI product behavior, support, and operations.
