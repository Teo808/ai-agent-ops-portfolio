What this demonstrates

I reproduce failure paths instead of guessing.

I separate provider, tool, memory, browser, and orchestration problems.

I write docs and setup notes that someone else can actually follow, not just notes for myself.

I turn real friction into product feedback engineers and support teams can act on.

What I would do first at an AI agent company

  • Load agent tools into real workflows and write down exactly where setup breaks down, not where it looks fine in a demo.
  • Build small repeatable checks for memory, tools, providers, browser steps, and fallback behavior so problems stay caught.
  • Take messy user reports and turn them into reproduction steps someone on the team can actually run.
  • Fix the docs and support notes where people keep getting stuck.
