Source and status handoff checklist

More context does not help support if nobody can tell where the answer came from, whether it is current, or who owns the next step.

The checklist

  • Source: What tool, policy, document, ticket, message, or record did the agent use?
  • Status: Is that source current, stale, blocked, missing, or waiting for approval?
  • Permission state: Was the agent allowed to act, asked to pause, or blocked by a real checkpoint?
  • Last action: What changed, if anything?
  • Open work: What still needs a human or the next run?
  • Owner: Who should check the next step?

Weak handoff versus useful handoff

Weak: "I checked the account." Better: "Checked the billing ticket and latest policy note."

Weak: "Looks good." Better: "Policy note is current. Billing ticket is still waiting on approval."

Weak: "Could not continue." Better: "Stopped because the action needed legal or account owner approval."

Weak: "User should review." Better: "Support should confirm approval, then resume from the billing step."

Why this matters

This is the support side of AI workflow QA. A clean handoff should reduce mystery. It should tell the next person what the agent used, what state the work is in, and what should happen next.

That habit matters for customer support, implementation, job application workflows, browser automation, and any product where an agent acts across real systems.
